Shipping Policy

At Giftastic by Archana, we take immense care in curating and packing your custom gift hampers to ensure they reach your loved ones safely and on time.

Our Courier Partners

We deliver Pan-India. To provide you with the best possible service, your orders are shipped via trusted delivery partners including DTDC, Professional Courier, or India Post.

Shipping Selection & Costs

The specific courier chosen for your order is carefully selected based on a few key factors:

  • Reachability: Which service has the most reliable network in your specific location.
  • Urgency: How quickly you need the hamper delivered.
  • Cost: Providing you with the most economical shipping option available.

Because of this, shipping timelines and delivery costs may vary slightly depending on the exact distance and location of the recipient.

Dispatch & Tracking

As soon as your custom gift hamper is handed over to our courier partner, we will immediately provide you with the dispatch proof and tracking details so you can monitor your package's journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay an advance to confirm my order?

Yes, to begin crafting your personalized hamper, we require an advance payment of 40% of the total order cost. Once the advance is received, our team will immediately start working on your beautiful gift!

Can I customize the items inside a hamper?

Absolutely! Every hamper can be fully customized to match your specific wishes and budget. There are no limitations—we are here to bring your perfect gifting vision to life.

How do I track my order?

As soon as your custom gift is dispatched, we will share the tracking details and proof of dispatch with you. You can easily track your package's journey directly through our courier partner's official website.
