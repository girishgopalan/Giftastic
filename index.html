Common Inquiries

Do I need to pay an advance to confirm my order? +
Yes, to begin crafting your personalized hamper, we require an advance payment of 40% of the total order value. This allows our artisans to commence work on your bespoke gift immediately.
Is it possible to fully customize the contents? +
Absolutely. Every hamper can be meticulously tailored to align with your specific preferences and budget. We invite you to use our 'Curate Hamper' tool to build your ideal arrangement.
How do I track my shipment? +
Upon dispatch, we will provide you with comprehensive tracking credentials. You can monitor your package's journey seamlessly via our premium courier partner's portal.
