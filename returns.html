Return Policy

Strict No-Return Policy: Because every Giftastic hamper is deeply personalized and customized specifically for your order, we do not accept returns, exchanges, or offer refunds. All sales are final.

Quality Assurance

We pride ourselves on delivering premium quality products. Before any order leaves our facility, the products are thoroughly inspected and reviewed by our team.

We ensure that the quality, design, and components align perfectly with the requirements shared by you, the requester, during the ordering process.

Dispatch & Delivery Proof

Once your order passes our quality check, it is securely packaged and handed over to our courier partners. We will provide you with solid proof of dispatch (including courier tracking details) the moment your gift is on its way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay an advance to confirm my order?

Yes, to begin crafting your personalized hamper, we require an advance payment of 40% of the total order cost. Once the advance is received, our team will immediately start working on your beautiful gift!

Can I customize the items inside a hamper?

Absolutely! Every hamper can be fully customized to match your specific wishes and budget. There are no limitations—we are here to bring your perfect gifting vision to life.

How do I track my order?

As soon as your custom gift is dispatched, we will share the tracking details and proof of dispatch with you. You can easily track your package's journey directly through our courier partner's official website.
